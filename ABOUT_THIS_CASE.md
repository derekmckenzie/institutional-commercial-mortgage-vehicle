# About This Case

This repository is a sanitized reconstruction of a proposed institutional commercial-mortgage structure.

## Public naming convention

Specific counterparties are intentionally replaced with functional descriptions:

- **Immediately Recognizable Canadian D-SIB**
- **Immediately Recognizable Canadian Life Insurer**
- **Affiliated Institutional Asset Manager**
- **Global Reinsurer / Reinsurance Provider**
- **Special-Purpose / Newly Created Insurer** where that working variant is relevant
- **External Legal & Tax Advisers**
- **Sponsor / Structuring Adviser**

## Evidence discipline

The repository uses three categories of statements:

1. **Documented mechanics** — directly present in the archived working materials.
2. **Project-author clarification** — context supplied during this reconstruction that is not explicitly stated in the archived decks / memos.
3. **Open questions** — accounting, regulatory or structuring items that would need to be resolved before execution.

These categories are kept separate so the case does not turn an intended outcome into a historical fact.

## What this case does not claim

It does not claim that:

- the proposed transaction closed;
- every contemplated counterparty executed the structure;
- the Canadian insurer had a balance-sheet problem;
- off-balance-sheet or derecognition treatment was achieved;
- regulatory-capital relief was achieved;
- the insurance / reinsurance contracting chain was finalized; or
- any historical return assumption should be read as a current investment expectation.
