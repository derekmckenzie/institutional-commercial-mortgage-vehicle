# Source Grounding

This file exists to keep the public reconstruction disciplined.

## Directly documented in the archived working materials

- MIE created to facilitate commercial-mortgage origination.
- Canadian life insurer provides origination, underwriting, servicing and administration.
- Affiliated asset manager provides investment management / mortgage selection within investment guidelines.
- MIE invests in partial interests with the Canadian insurer.
- MIE interests are pari passu with the insurer.
- Canadian D-SIB contemplates an approximately C$300 million Private Note Issuance Facility.
- Notes can be issued for each mortgage origination.
- Note term and principal / interest cash flows can be matched to expected mortgage cash flows.
- PNIF helps address uncertain mortgage-origination timing.
- MIE has an invest / pass decision point approximately 14 days before closing.
- If MIE passes, insurer can take down the mortgage independently.
- Insurance / reinsurance is contemplated against mortgage payment failure.
- Reinsurance provider may earn premiums and gain diversification / covariance benefits.
- A more detailed working variant uses a newly created insurer that purchases reinsurance.
- Detailed investment guidelines include first-priority mortgages, maximum 60% LTV, DSCR, appraisal, diversification and other credit controls.
- Regulatory and tax workstreams were being examined.
- A potential consolidation issue was identified for one proposed MIE owner based on power / variable-interest considerations.

## Project-author clarification used in this repository

- One intended benefit was for the MIE's mortgage share to be separately owned / funded rather than retained on the Canadian insurer's balance sheet, where commercial-mortgage exposure was capital-intensive.

## Explicitly not claimed

- The insurer had a balance-sheet problem.
- Balance-sheet capacity was the reason the insurer participated.
- The insurer needed or intended to originate more mortgages.
- Off-balance-sheet treatment was achieved.
- Derecognition was achieved.
- Regulatory-capital relief was achieved.
- Reinsurance had a finalized attachment point or loss waterfall.
- The Global Reinsurer necessarily contracted directly with the MIE in the final structure.
